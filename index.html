Song Cards

Turn a Spotify playlist into printable cards: a QR code on the front, artist, year and title on the back.

1. Playlist

Spotify dates each song by the album it's on, so songs from compilations and remasters often get the wrong year, like a 1980 hit dated 2008 because it's on a "Best of". With this on, every song is also looked up in Apple's iTunes catalog and the free MusicBrainz music database, and the earliest year found goes on the card.

Those services only allow a limited number of lookups per minute, so this takes about 3 seconds per song (around 5 minutes for 100 songs). You can review and download while it runs. Results are remembered in this browser, so loading the same songs again is instant. Song titles and artists are sent to Apple and MusicBrainz for the lookup. Turn this off to use Spotify's dates right away.

Spotify only lets this app read playlists you own or collaborate on. For someone else's playlist, copy it first in Spotify with “⋯ → Add to other playlist → New playlist”.

2. Check the songs

Type in the Year on card column to correct a year; clear it to go back to the suggestion. Your corrections are remembered in this browser.

3. Print

  1. Print double-sided, flipping on the edge you chose above.
  2. Print at actual size (100%), not “fit to page”, so fronts and backs line up.
  3. Each A4 sheet holds 12 cards of 6.5 × 6.5 cm. Cut along the lines.
